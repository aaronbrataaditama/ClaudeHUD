ClaudeHUD — UI design suggestion

A 4 px light strip on the screen edge. Hover it for 250 ms and the detail panel slides out from the strip. This is a design mockup: the real panel is drawn natively with Direct2D, and the HTML is only here to show the layout. Spec: PLAN-CLAUDEHUD.md §2.2–2.3, §4.

1 · Top edge, Team plan (default placement)

Interactive. Click the pin (or the strip) to unpin, then move away and hover the strip again to see the reveal. Hover a session name to see its working folder. Click a session to collapse/expand its sub-agents.

2 · Left edge, Enterprise plan with spend limit

The panel slides right from the strip. For Enterprise the header shows spend, and the usage block shows the monthly spend meter instead of the 5-hour and weekly windows. claude.ai is degraded here, which is shown in the footer but does not turn the strip red.

3 · Other states

Limit reached: strip red, no glow. The banner says when turns will work again.

No sessions: strip off. Opened from the tray icon; usage and status still shown.

4 · The light

Priority red › yellow › amber › green › off. Solid colours with no animation; busy vs idle is a brightness step. The colours pass the palette validator's colour-blind and normal-vision separation checks on this surface.

5 · Tray icon and menu

A native Shell_NotifyIcon icon with a native TrackPopupMenu menu, drawn by Windows so it matches the system exactly. Left-click the icon to show/hide the panel (and acknowledge a latched red). Hover for the tooltip. Right-click for the menu. In the mockup below, hover the icon to see the tooltip and hover "Edge" / "Monitor" / "Warn at" to see the submenus.

Menu order: the panel toggle (bold, the default action), placement settings, startup, status page, Exit. Settings apply immediately and are saved to claudehud.settings.json.

Icon and tooltip per state

The pixel creature from the app icon, drawn at runtime from a 12 × 7 pixel map, with a status badge in the light's colour (bottom-right, with a 1 px cut-out ring so it separates from the body). Off = grey creature, no badge. Shown at 16 px (100%) and 32 px (200% scaling). The tooltip is two lines, at most 127 characters: the reason for the colour, then counts and usage.

6 · App icon

Source: assets/ClaudeHUD_icon.jpg. The full artwork is used at 32 px and above (exe icon in Explorer, panel header, first-run). Below 32 px the orbits and balls blur into noise, so the small sizes use a simplified tile: the same indigo→violet background with only the pixel creature. The tray does not use the tile; it uses the bare creature plus status badge (§5).
